## Current Research Direction

I am especially interested in models that have to survive messy data: noisy labels, temporal drift, ambiguous language, confounding, and uncertain decisions. My strongest current thread is temporal stance analysis with retrieval, weak supervision, and calibrated evaluation.

## Evaluation Style

I try not to stop at one accuracy number. I look for calibration curves, confidence or credible intervals, ablations, subgroup checks, temporal validation, posterior predictive checks, and decision-threshold tradeoffs.
